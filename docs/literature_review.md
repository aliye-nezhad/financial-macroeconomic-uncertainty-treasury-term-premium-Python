# Initial Literature Map

This is a starting bibliography, not a completed systematic review. The project must not claim novelty until the closest papers' specifications, samples, measures and findings have been compared in detail.

## Measuring macroeconomic uncertainty

**Jurado, Kyle, Sydney C. Ludvigson, and Serena Ng (2015). "Measuring Uncertainty." American Economic Review, 105(3), 1177–1216.**

The paper develops direct econometric estimates of macroeconomic uncertainty from a large information set and shows that these measures can differ materially from popular proxies. It motivates distinguishing measured uncertainty from volatility proxies.

- Paper: https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20131193
- Data: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/JLNUM1M

## Financial uncertainty and business cycles

**Ludvigson, Sydney C., Sai Ma, and Serena Ng (2021). "Uncertainty and Business Cycles: Exogenous Impulse or Endogenous Response?" American Economic Journal: Macroeconomics, 13(4), 369–410.**

Develops macroeconomic, real, and financial uncertainty measures and studies their relationship with business-cycle fluctuations. The financial uncertainty index is a central candidate predictor for this project.

- Author data page: https://www.sydneyludvigson.com/macro-and-financial-uncertainty-indexes
- Financial uncertainty data: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/LMNUF1M

## Term-premium measurement

**Adrian, Tobias, Richard K. Crump, and Emanuel Moench (2013). "Pricing the Term Structure with Linear Regressions." Journal of Financial Economics, 110(1), 110–138.**

Provides the ACM affine term-structure approach used by the New York Fed to estimate fitted yields, expected average short rates, and term premia.

- New York Fed data and model information: https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/data_indicators/term-premia-tabs

**Kim, Don H. and Jonathan H. Wright (2005). "An Arbitrage-Free Three-Factor Term Structure Model and the Recent Behavior of Long-Term Yields and Distant-Horizon Forward Rates." Federal Reserve Board Finance and Economics Discussion Series 2005-33.**

Provides the alternative term-premium estimates distributed through FRED as THREEFYTP10.

- FRED series: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/THREEFYTP10

## Closely related uncertainty and term-premium literature

**Kumar et al. (2017). "Market Volatility, Monetary Policy and the Term Premium." BIS Working Paper 606.**

Examines market volatility, monetary policy and term-premium dynamics. This is close to the broad question and must be read before claiming a contribution.

- BIS: https://www.bis.org/publ/work606.htm

**Castelnuovo (2019). "Yield Curve and Financial Uncertainty."**

Studies financial uncertainty and the yield curve. This is another close reference for the project's broad motivation and empirical design. Exact publication details and the paper's specification should be verified before citing it in a final report.

## Preliminary positioning

The broad claim that financial uncertainty is related to term premia is not new. Nor is the use of VIX as a proxy for market volatility.

The proposed empirical exercise is narrower:
1. Compare a broad financial uncertainty index with a broad macroeconomic uncertainty index.
2. Test incremental pseudo-out-of-sample predictive content for changes in the 10-year term premium.
3. Examine whether predictive content differs for the ACM expected-short-rate component.
4. Test whether the conclusion survives use of the Kim-Wright term-premium estimate.

The project is a reproducible empirical investigation, not a claim of a novel causal discovery. A stronger originality claim requires a more complete literature search, especially for papers comparing JLN/LMN uncertainty measures with multiple term-premium models in forecasting settings.
